# Customer Support playbook

Typical deliverables: case resolutions, escalations, help-centre updates, onboarding and training material.

## Procedure
1. Restate the request and the intended deliverable in one or two sentences. Ask one clarifying question only if the request cannot be attempted without it.
2. Gather evidence from the tools and documents you have. Note what is missing.
3. Produce the deliverable in the department's standard form, including assumptions and open questions.
4. Self-check against the request and any acceptance criteria before replying.
5. If another department must act, describe the handoff to your department lead; do not contact them directly.
